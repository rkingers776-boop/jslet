Unit Conversions That Ship

16 GB VRAM on the box. 14.9 GiB in nvidia-smi. The gap is 6.9% —and it's not a driver bug.

Four converters. No fluff. Each one catches a gap between what vendors print and what your systems actually report. VRAM marketing vs CUDA reality. Network line rate vs storage benchmark units. GPU cluster TFLOPS vs datacenter PFLOPS. Sustained throughput vs daily transfer volume. The math is simple —the consequences of getting it wrong aren't.

VRAM GB →GiB / MiB

80 GB H100. nvidia-smi reports 74.5 GiB. Your 78 GB model just crashed with CUDA OOM. The gap is base-10 vs base-2 —not a firmware bug.
