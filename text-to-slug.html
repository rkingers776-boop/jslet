Titles become SEO-friendly URL slugs in real time. English and Chinese, configurable separator and casing, live length check — plus batch mode for whole blogs.

🟢📖 Slug Best Practices This Tool Bakes In

Drop stop words. "How to Compress Images for the Web" → how-to-compress-images-for-the-web keeps the meaning while compress-images-web keeps only the signal. The tool strips a, an, the, of, and, or, for, to, in, on, with, how when they sit between real words — the length check shows you the saving.

60 characters is the truncation line. Google cuts URLs in search results around 60 characters. The live counter turns amber past 60 so you see the exact moment your slug loses its message in the SERP.

Hyphens, not underscores. Google treats hyphens as word separators and underscores as word joiners — webp-vs-jpg is three words to the indexer, webp_vs_jpg is one. Hyphen is the default for a reason.

Chinese titles: two strategies. Pinyin (ru-he-ya-suo-tu-pian) for international reach, raw Chinese for domestic SEO. Compare both live with the pinyin toggle before you publish — changing a slug later breaks old links.
