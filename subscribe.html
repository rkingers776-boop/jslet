🟢🔒 Why No Newsletter?

Email newsletters require you to trust us with your inbox. They require us to run a mail server, manage a subscriber database, and write a privacy policy that says "we don't track you" while SendGrid logs every open. RSS has none of these problems. You pull. We don't push. It's the subscription model the web was built on, and it still works perfectly.
