🟢📖 About Lorem Ipsum

Lorem Ipsum is the industry-standard placeholder text used by designers, developers, and typographers since the 1500s. It is derived from a passage of Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum (On the Ends of Good and Evil), written in 45 BC.

The text is used because it approximates the natural distribution of letters and word lengths in English, without the distraction of readable content. This lets reviewers focus on the visual design rather than the copy.

This generator uses a deterministic Latin-derived word list — no external API calls, no randomness that breaks reproducibility. Every generation with the same settings produces the same output, making it ideal for automated tests and screenshot comparisons.
